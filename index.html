HOW TO PLAYREAD THE RUN AS 8 SIMPLE SYSTEMS
FIELD GUIDE
THE LOOPMOVE → COLLECT → BUILD → FIGHT → SURVIVE 05:00
01MOVE & SURVIVE
WASDARROWS

Change direction. Avoid your body, arena walls and active threats.

PHONE

Swipe directly on the arena to steer. Touch the ability cards for DASH, SHIELD, PULSE and FURY.

3 LIVES

Collision costs a life. A MEDKIT can restore one when available.

02ABILITIES
SPACE

DASH — burst forward and cut through danger.

SHIFT

SHIELD — temporary damage protection.

E

PULSE — hit nearby hunters, hazards and the Warden.

Q

FURY — charge to 100%, then trigger 2X SCORE + threat wipe.

03BUILD YOUR RUN
ENERGY

Green cells give score, XP, FURY and growth. Keep collecting without losing the chain.

CORE

High-value pickup that accelerates score and XP progression.

CARDS

Collectible EVOLUTION CARDS add XP, score and small build bonuses. Their collection persists.

XP

Level up your SNAKE BUILD from LVL 1 to LVL 10.

04THREAT SYSTEM
HAZARDS

Red danger tiles force routing decisions. Near misses can reward careful movement.

HUNTERS

Enemies increase pressure. ELITES are tougher; CHARGER and WATCHER variants behave differently.

WARDEN

Boss encounter with 3 phases. LOCKDOWN is the final pressure state.

TELEGRAPHS

Attack markers give you a short warning before high-risk impacts.

05MISSION SYSTEMS
OBJECTIVE

Every run gets a target. Finish it for score, XP and FURY.

CONTRACT

Accept a high-value contract for extra rewards; REDLINE adds threat while active.

MUTATION

Each run rolls a rule set such as SALVAGE RUN, HUNTER ALERT or OVERCLOCK.

CHAIN

Collect energy, cores or enemies before the decay window empties.

06RISK / REWARD
SUPPLY

Later-zone drops create a danger ring but can provide rare emergency rewards.

EXTRACT

From 2:00 onward, CASH OUT secures a bonus or KEEP RUNNING adds temporary heat.

SHRINE

Risk Shrine raises temporary threat for a larger reward.

VAULT

A rare portal opens a short SECRET VAULT loot surge.

07PROGRESSION
UPGRADES

Finished runs award DATA for permanent DASH, CORE, FURY and ARMOR upgrades.

ACHIEVEMENTS

Long-term milestones are tracked across runs.

PERFECT

30 seconds without damage activates a temporary 1.25X score bonus.

BOUNTY

One enemy can be marked as the run target for an extra reward.

08MODES & PAUSE
P / ESC

Pause at any time. Switching tabs also auto-pauses the run.

DAILY

Date-seeded rules with a dedicated local leaderboard and ghost route.

ENDLESS

After 05:00 the run cycles again with higher heat.

GOAL

Survive the lockdown, evolve your build, defeat the Warden and set a record.
