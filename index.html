HOW TO PLAY

MOVE WASD / ARROWS — Phone: direction buttons or swipe.

DASH SPACE — burst forward.

SHIELD SHIFT — block damage briefly.

PULSE E — clear nearby threats and damage the WARDEN.

FURY Q — charge the meter, then activate 2X SCORE + threat wipe.

LIFE You have 3 lives. A hit removes one. MEDKIT can restore one life.

XP ENERGY, CORE and enemy takedowns build XP and level you up. Level-up restores one LIFE.

THREAT The danger meter rises with zone, hunters, hazards and survival time. Critical heat means the run is becoming lethal.

ELITE HUNTER Elite Hunters appear later, move more aggressively and require two PULSE hits to defeat.

STREAK Chain milestones at x5, x10 and x15 grant bonus score, XP and FURY.

OBJECTIVE Every run gets a different target. Complete it for score, XP and FURY charge.

CONTRACT One high-value contract is assigned every run. REDLINE increases THREAT while active; clear the contract for extra score, XP and FURY.

SUPPLY DROP Drops appear in later zones, create a hazard ring and expire after 14 seconds. RARE and LEGENDARY drops give stronger emergency rewards.

MUTATIONS Every run rolls one rule-set: SALVAGE RUN, HUNTER ALERT, HAZARD SHIFT, SUPPLY RUSH or OVERCLOCK. Each changes risk, spawn rate or rewards.

EXTRACTION From 2:00 onward, extraction windows pause the run. CASH OUT secures a score bonus; KEEP RUNNING adds temporary heat and unlocks another window later.

RISK CONTRACTS At run start, ACCEPT adds threat and enables the contract reward. DECLINE removes the contract for that run.

CHAIN Combo has a live decay window. Collect energy, cores or enemies before the bar empties to preserve your chain.

CHARGER / WATCHER Elite variants behave differently: CHARGER bursts toward you; WATCHER backs away and raises threat.

WARDEN The boss now has 3 phases. Phase 3 enters LOCKDOWN and can teleport.

ZONE EVENTS HAZARD STORM, HUNTER SWARM, POWER SURGE, BLACKOUT, DOUBLE CORE and HUNTER LOCK temporarily alter the rules.

UPGRADES Finish runs to earn DATA for permanent DASH, CORE, FURY and ARMOR upgrades.

ACHIEVEMENTS Long-term milestones are tracked across runs.

DAILY RUN Uses a date-seeded rule set and has its own local leaderboard and ghost route.

ENDLESS MODE After each five-minute cycle, the run continues with higher heat and another cycle.

PROCEDURAL ARENA Each zone gets new blocking cells. Hitting one costs a LIFE.

TELEGRAPHS Dangerous attacks show a warning marker before/around impact.

PERFECT RUN After 30 seconds without damage, score rewards gain a temporary 1.25x bonus. Any hit breaks it.

BOUNTY TARGET One enemy is marked during each run. Destroy it for an extra reward.

RISK SHRINE Optional pickup adds temporary threat for a large reward.

SECRET VAULT Rare portal leads to a short loot surge.

SOUND Arcade feedback uses lightweight WebAudio cues and can be disabled in OPTIONS.

RUN ANALYSIS End screen reports death cause, maximum threat, condition and mode.

GOAL Survive 5 minutes, evolve, build combo, hunt enemies, destroy the WARDEN.

APEX CORE Rare power-up: massive score, combo boost and hazard wipe.

QUICK PAUSE Tap PAUSE or press P / ESC. The game automatically pauses when you switch tabs; resume manually when ready.

ZONE CHECKPOINT Reaching a new sector grants bonus score, XP, FURY and a short shield.

NEAR MISS Pass within one tile of a hazard or hunter to earn a small bonus. The reward has a short cooldown to prevent farming.

PHONE Landscape gives the best control layout.
